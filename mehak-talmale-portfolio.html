AmericasNLP 2026, under review

Culturally Aware Image Captioning for Indigenous Languages (CICIL)

Captioning models tend to describe everything through a Western lens and fail on languages with little training data. With a team of four, I co-designed a pipeline that grounds each caption in retrieved cultural context, checks it for false claims, and only then translates it.

  1. RetrieveSigLIP and MiniLM find relevant cultural context
  2. VerifyQwen2.5-VL rejects claims the image doesn't support
  3. TranslateGemini 2.5 on Vertex AI into 5 languages
82%preferred over baseline
κ = 0.79inter-rater agreement
7B vs 2Bonly the 7B verifier caught false claims
